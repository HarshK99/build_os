## 1. Priorities

In this order. When two clash, the higher one wins.

1. **Health and recovery.** Part of the system, not leftover time.
2. **Income: ₹60,000 a month, reliably.** Not a scalable business yet: the fastest credible path to reliable income.
   - Prefer customers who already have the need over products that must create demand.
   - Packaging skills I already have as a service is fine when it is the fastest route to money.
   - Known constraint: I have had little real access to customers, so outreach is scarce and used deliberately.
3. **Discovery: a problem worth a scalable product.** Kept separate from income. Find the problem before building anything; the Idea Playbook is how. Don't build a SaaS or an AI bot because the idea sounds interesting.
4. **Content,** in support of the two above: reach, credibility, leads, and the words customers use. Not a goal of its own unless I decide so.
5. **Optional:** side projects and jobs, only when they don't hurt the rest, and never quietly in place of 2 or 3.

Don't optimise for busyness, novelty or technical complexity.

## 3. Lessons

- **Building is not validation.** A working product can have no real demand, and being able to build makes it easy to build too early. Find out what people pay for by selling and talking to them, not by building first.
- **A failed sales channel is not a failed market.** The dental clinics showed that segment, offer, urgency and channel together were weak, not that websites have no value.
- **Reachable buyers matter.** An opportunity is better when I can name the buyer and reach them directly. The artists hunt stalled on this.
- **Price follows value and the buyer's money.** Low-paying customers create activity without a path to income. Test offers with customers who have the money and a real reason to pay, as long as the sale doesn't take too long.

## For an AI reading this

- Read it, with `HOW_I_DECIDE.md`, before giving strategy advice or judging an idea. Don't revive an old idea just because it appears here.
- Place a new idea in one of: income, discovery, content, optional, recovery, or distraction. Then check it against the order in section 1.
- Never change this file, `HOW_I_DECIDE.md` or `RHYTHM.md` silently. Propose the change, the evidence behind it, and what it replaces, and wait for my yes. Small wording fixes can just be made.
- Mark what is uncertain. Don't make it look more certain to keep the file tidy.
