# Rhythm

How my days and weeks run. Read when planning a day or a week, not for strategy; that is in `FOUNDER_OS.md`.

## 1. Work modes

Sorted by how much thinking the work needs, not by whether it happens on a computer.

| Mode | For | Examples | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep** | Thinking, judgment, deciding | Strategy, problem discovery, important product decisions, a customer call where I adapt as I go | Gets the high-energy hours |
| **Production** | Turning decisions into output | Build, design, write, edit, ship | Only once things are clear enough |
| **Light** | Low-effort work | Research, admin, posting, organising, routine messages, collecting a list of customers | Never takes the best hours just because it is easy to start |

## 2. Energy and schedule

- Sleep at least 7 hours. Exercise 1 hour a day, not negotiable.
- Wake around 7:30 AM; start work around 8:00 AM.
- Best energy roughly 8–11 AM; second window 11 AM–1 PM.
- After lunch is lower; 2–5 PM is workable but lower-energy; 5–6 PM is a natural break.
- Dinner around 7:30 PM; gym around 9–10 PM; no planned work after the gym.
- Aim to sleep around midnight rather than 1 AM.

These are working assumptions. Change them when the schedule has really changed for a while.

## 3. Weekly rhythm

- **Outcomes:** what must be true by the end of the week, tied to the priorities in `FOUNDER_OS.md`. For example: progress toward ₹60k, a set number of customer or problem tests, a piece of content if it supports them.
- **Bottleneck:** the one thing most holding progress back, not a list of every problem.
- **Experiments:** each with its hypothesis, action, the evidence sought, and what I will decide once I have it. The loop is in `HOW_I_DECIDE.md`.
- **Review:** what happened, what I learned, what changed, what to stop, continue, and test next.

Outcomes, not a long task list.
