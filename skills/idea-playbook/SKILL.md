# Idea Playbook

The playbook is one file. Read it before doing anything else in this task:

`D:\FounderMode\build_os\IDEA_PLAYBOOK.md`

It builds on my shared instructions, How I Work, which you already have.

Then:

1. **Find the step and the route.** Look in `D:\FounderMode\ideation\` for the folder of the hunt I mean, and work out which of the six steps it has reached from `research.md` and `directions.md`. The route is on the first line of `research.md`; a hunt with none written is route 1. With no folder yet, it is step A, and I pick the route first: complaints or funded startups. Tell me the step and the route in one line.
2. **Do one step,** using that step's section, and stop at its gate.
3. **Keep to rule 1.** Ask nothing about me as a person. The only questions about me are what I know and who I can reach.
4. **Step B uses the `customer-research` skill.** Quotes stay exactly as written, each with its link.
5. **Hold the gate at step E.** No project, product-sense pass or building until three conversations are written into `research.md`. Don't offer a way around it. Draft the messages so sending them is easy. One landing page is allowed before the gate, under the limits in step E; nothing else is.
6. **Report what didn't fit.** If a step felt long or the playbook was wrong for this hunt, tell me, so it gets corrected.

If the file cannot be read, say so and stop. Don't work from memory of it.
